A fragmented, low-trust marketplace

Two sides, waiting on the same missing piece.

Directories list vendors. They don't verify quality, guarantee delivery, or protect either side financially — so both sides are left carrying the risk alone.

The Couple
  • No transparency into a photographer's real track record — reviews are easily gamed, portfolios curated or borrowed.
  • Fake or misleading portfolios, with no verification of authorship or actual delivery.
  • Pricing confusion — inconsistent packages make comparing vendors apples-to-apples nearly impossible.
  • No recourse if a photographer cancels last-minute, loses data, or delivers late.
The Photographer
  • High customer acquisition cost — heavy reliance on word-of-mouth or expensive ad spend.
  • No structured tools for contracts, milestone payments, or delivery workflow — managed manually over chat.
  • Payment risk — couples delaying or disputing final payment after delivery, with no neutral escrow mechanism.
The terms of the promise

Seven clauses, not seven features.

Each one exists because a directory listing, left alone, has failed a couple or a photographer before.

01
Matching

AI Matching Engine

A guided questionnaire on style, budget, location, date, and coverage hours returns a ranked shortlist of the top 5 photographers — not an endless scroll.

02
Identity

Verification Badge

Identity, business registration, and portfolio-authorship checks — completed before a photographer is ever listed as "Verified."

03
Scoring

Composite Trust Scores

Portfolio Quality, On-Time Delivery, and Customer Satisfaction — each computed from verified, platform-tracked data, not open-ended reviews.

04
Payment

Escrow Payments

Couples pay into escrow at booking. Funds release on milestone completion: booking confirmed, event completed, gallery delivered.

05
Timeline

Delivery Workflow

A shared timeline for shoot date, editing milestones, and final delivery — visible to both the couple and the photographer.

06
Guarantee

Protection Plan

Coverage for cancellation, data loss, and delayed delivery, with a defined refund policy — the safety net for a day with no re-shoot.

07
Pricing

Standardized Packages

Photographers map their offerings to common tiers — hours, deliverables, albums, second shooter — so couples compare like-for-like.
